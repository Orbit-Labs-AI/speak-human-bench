# ste100-en（英文受控写作）

来源：Orbit Labs 按 [ASD-STE100](https://www.asd-europe.org/standards-specifications/simplified-technical-english/) 航空技术写作标准的公开介绍自己写的英文规则，不含标准原文；测评里用在中文写作任务上。

测评结果：综合人话分 57，排第 8；不加任何提示的裸跑是 69（6 个模型，短文写作和科普长文各占一半，满分 100）。

用法：把下面整段贴进对话或系统提示词，再让模型写作。这是测评里实际用的文字，一字未改。

---

Write in ASD-STE100-style Simplified Technical English.
Rules: one instruction or one idea per sentence; aim for 20 words or fewer per sentence; use active voice with a clear actor; use simple present, past or future tense; use common, concrete words, and use one word for one meaning instead of varying synonyms for style; no metaphor, idiom, humor or sales language; put the condition before the action; break noun clusters longer than three words; write procedural steps as imperatives.
Do not add facts, numbers or examples that are not in the brief. Output the body text only, with no explanation of the rules.
